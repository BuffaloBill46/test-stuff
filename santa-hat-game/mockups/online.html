About the game

Controls

Computer: WASD or arrows to move, click to throw where you point, Space to auto-aim, 1–4 for emotes.

Phone: drag on the left side to move, tap on the right side to throw.

Game types

Free-for-all: everyone chases whoever wears the hat.

Nice vs Naughty: two teams; team points add up.

A match is 3 rounds of 90 seconds. Most points wins.

Scoring

Wearing the hat10 / sec

Knocking the hat off someone25

How ranking works

  1. 10 free tickets a day. They reset every 24 hours.
  2. 1 ticket per ranked match. Leave before it starts and you get it back.
  3. Every real player's ticket adds 10 points to the pot. Bots don't add points or take a paid place.
  4. Paid places. 3 or fewer real players: 1st takes the whole pot. 4 or more: 1st gets 60%, 2nd 20%, 3rd 20%.
  5. Not placing costs 5 points.
  6. The leaderboard is your total points earned, from ranked matches and special events.
  7. Need more tickets? Buy up to 10 extra every 24 hours in the Store.
