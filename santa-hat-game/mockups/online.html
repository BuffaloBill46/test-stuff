About the game

Controls

Computer: WASD or arrows to move, click to throw where you point, Space to auto-aim, 1–4 for emotes.

Phone: drag on the left side to move, tap on the right side to throw.

Game types

Free-for-all: everyone chases whoever wears the hat.

Nice vs Naughty: two teams; team points add up.

A match is 3 rounds of 90 seconds. Most points wins.

Scoring

Wearing the hat10 / sec
Catching the flying hat on your head50
Knocking the hat off someone25
Any snowball hit5

How ranking works

  1. 10 free tickets a day. They reset every 24 hours.
  2. 1 ticket per ranked match. Leave before it starts and you get it back.
  3. Every player in the match adds 10 points to the pot, bots included.
  4. Paid places. 3 or fewer players: only 1st is paid and takes the whole pot. 4 or more: 1st gets 60%, 2nd 20%, 3rd 20%.
  5. Bots play to win. Matches can include bots under player names, and they can take paid places.
  6. Not placing costs 5 points.
  7. The leaderboard is your total points earned, from ranked matches and special events.
  8. Need more tickets? Buy up to 10 extra every 24 hours in the Store.

            How to win

            1. Match 3, 4 or 5 in a row in a straight line or a diagonal, always starting on the first (left) reel. There are 11 lines.
            2. Santa Hats are wild: they stand in for any symbol except Coal.
            3. Every Santa Hat anywhere on the grid pays 6¢, win or not.
            4. All winning lines add up on the same pull.
            5. Fill all 25 squares with Santa Hats for the pool jackpot (a share of the whole pool).
