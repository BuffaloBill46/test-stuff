          About the game

          Read the full player guide: the snowball fight, levels, special snowballs, gear, ranked, the games for SANTA and the lottery.

          Controls

          Computer: WASD or arrows to move, click to throw where you point, Space to auto-aim, 1–4 for emotes.

          Phone: move with the joystick, tap anywhere else to throw there. To move the joystick, triple-tap it and hold, then drag it where you like.

          Special snowballs: tap one (or press Q, E, R) for your next throw. Double-tap to lock it on: every throw uses it until you double-tap it again.

          Game types

          Free-for-all: everyone chases whoever wears the hat.

          Nice vs Naughty: two teams; team points add up.

          A match is one 60-second round. Most points wins.

          Scoring

          Wearing the hat10 / sec
          Catching the flying hat on your head50
          Knocking the hat off someone25
          Any snowball hit5

          How ranking works

          1. 10 free tickets a day. They refill every day at 9 PM Eastern (Indiana time).
          2. 1 ticket per ranked match. Leave before it starts and you get it back.
          3. Every player adds 2 points per level to the pot (level 1 adds 2, level 10 adds 20); each bot adds 5. Beating higher levels wins you more.
          4. Paid places. 3 or fewer players: only 1st is paid and takes the whole pot. 4 or more: 1st gets 60%, 2nd 20%, 3rd 20%.
          5. Bots play to win. Every match has at least 2 bots (under player names) unless 8 real players fill it, and they can take paid places.
          6. Not placing costs 5 points.
          7. The leaderboard is your total points earned, from ranked matches and special events.
          8. Need more tickets? Buy up to 10 extra a day in the Store (that limit resets at 9 PM too).
