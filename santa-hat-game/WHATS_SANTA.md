hat. Then the game never dropped them again. Within a few years the hat that had been free was worth more than a hundred
million gp: the rare you bought before you could afford a partyhat. In RuneScape 3 the original still trades for hundreds of
millions of gp.

**$SANTA is that hat, on-chain.**
