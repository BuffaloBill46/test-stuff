======================================================================================================================
HOW TO GET THE MOST VIEWS (what matters most first)
======================================================================================================================
1. Upload the video file straight to X (the MP4). Never post a YouTube/Drive link instead: X shows native video bigger
   and autoplays it in the feed.
2. One post a day, not all 12 at once. Each one gets its own day in the feed. Suggested order is below.
3. Post when people are scrolling: try around 12–2 PM and 7–9 PM Eastern. Weekday evenings for game content.
4. Put the website link in your FIRST REPLY, not the main post. X tends to show posts with links to fewer people.
   (Easy test: do it for half the posts and compare views.) The ready-made reply line is under each post.
5. Ask for a reply. The 5 "coming soon?" videos end with "Reply YES". Replies are the biggest push X gives a post.
   Answer the first few replies yourself within the first hour: that keeps it climbing.
6. The first second decides. Every video opens with a big line of text, and every word is on screen, because most
   people scroll with the sound off.
7. Pin your best one to your profile (watch which gets the most replies after a few days).
8. 1–2 tags at most: $SANTA and #Solana. More looks like spam.
9. Reply to bigger Solana / gaming accounts with the right clip (e.g. the tournament one under a "what are you playing"
   post). Quote-post your own older videos when you post a related one.
