Snowball Square

Up to 8 players in a snowy ring. One Santa hat. Wear it to score; knock it off everyone else.

Controls

  • Computer: WASD or arrows to move, click to throw where you point, Space to auto-aim, 1–4 for emotes, Q E R for your special snowballs: press once to throw one, press twice quickly to lock it on (every throw uses it; press twice again for plain snowballs).
  • Phone: move with the joystick, tap anywhere else to throw there. To move the joystick, triple-tap it and hold, then drag it where you like. Your special snowballs are the buttons under your snowball counter: tap one for your next throw, or double-tap to lock it on (a gold ring and LOCKED show; every throw uses it until you double-tap it again). Run short of snowballs for it and that throw is a plain one; the lock stays.

A match

  • One round of 60 seconds. Most points at the end wins.
  • Free-for-all: everyone chases whoever wears the hat. Nice vs Naughty: two teams, and team points add up.
  • A snowball hit knocks a player down for a moment. Hit whoever wears the hat and it flies off; catch it on your head to take it.
  • Your snowball counter refills by itself, and much faster when you stand next to a snow pile.

Scoring

Playing with friends

  • Your own room: make a room and share its code or link. Bots fill the empty spots and the host starts the match. These matches are just for fun: they don't count for levels or the daily tasks.
  • Auto match together: the host of a friends' room can take everyone into a public match in one tap. The game holds seats for your whole group while you move over, and public matches count.
  • Games waiting for players: the Play page lists public matches that are filling up right now. Press Join to hop into one.

Special snowballs

Bought once in the Store and kept. Put them in your special slots on the Avatar screen (more slots open as you level up). Each throw uses snowballs from your counter.

Tap a special's button (or Q E R) to use it for your next throw. Double-tap to lock it on for every throw; double-tap again for normal snowballs.

Try before you buy: in the Store, Try it opens a free practice match with that snowball already loaded.

Special gear

Gear changes how you play: more hits before you're knocked down, more snowballs, more speed. Wear it in your gear slot on the Avatar screen.

No stacking:

Wears out: gear lasts 7 days from the first match you wear it in. Then you can buy it again.

Levels and looks

Each level sets how many snowballs you start a match with, and how many special snowball and gear slots you have.

Looks you unlock

Skin tones are free for everyone. Three faces are sold on the Avatar screen; every other look unlocks as you level up, and levels 5 and 10 each give a full costume.
