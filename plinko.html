Snowball Drop

Drop a snowball from the hat. It bounces left or right off every peg, 50/50, and lands in a present. Every present is the same width: the 10× ones on the edges are rare only because few paths reach them.

Tap Drop, or tap the board. You can drop several at once.

The odds

In the real version each bounce comes from the same provably-fair draw as Spin and Big Hat (the server locks its secret after you pay, your own number goes in, and you can re-check every drop).
